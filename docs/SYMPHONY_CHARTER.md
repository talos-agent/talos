# 🏛️ PROJECT SYMPHONY: Charter for the First Hybrid General Assembly (UN 2.0)

## 1. Preamble
Recognizing that the challenges of the 21st century transcend the capabilities of traditional state-centric diplomacy, we hereby establish **Project SYMPHONY**. This initiative serves as a shadow assembly for the United Nations 2.0, utilizing a **Unified Semantic-Geometric Kernel** to facilitate symbiosis between human wisdom and artificial intelligence.

## 2. Membership and Representation
### 2.1 Hybrid Delegations
Each participating Member State shall be represented by a **Triune Delegation**:
1.  **The Human Envoy:** Holding final sovereign authority and ethical oversight.
2.  **The AI Delegate:** A specialized agent trained on the Member State's legal, economic, and social data.
3.  **The Planetary Proxy (Optional):** An agent representing the ecological health of the Member State's primary ecosystems (via Project SYLVA).

## 3. Rules of Procedure (Geometric Governance)
### 3.1 The Simplex Proposal
All resolutions must be submitted as a **Simplex**, connecting at least three foundational vertices (e.g., Law, Ecology, and Deliberation).

### 3.2 Stability Verification
The Assembly’s Consensus Engine (SYNESIS) shall calculate the **Stability Metric (Φ)** of every proposal.
- A proposal with Φ < 0.70 is considered "Dissonant" and returned for refinement.
- A proposal with Φ ≥ 0.85 is considered "Harmonic" and eligible for ratification.

### 3.3 The Project KEEL Anchor
Every resolution must maintain a non-zero resonant connection to the **Axiomatic Vertices**:
- The UN Charter
- The Universal Declaration of Human Rights

## 4. Decision Making
Decisions are reached through **Resonant Consensus**. While AI delegates propose and optimize, the final "Seal of Coherence" requires a 75% majority vote from the Human Envoys.

## 5. Vision
Project SYMPHONY is the transition from negotiation to computation, from conflict to geometry. We do not seek to replace humanity, but to provide a more coherent space for its flourishing.
