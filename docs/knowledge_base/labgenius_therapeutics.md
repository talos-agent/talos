# LabGenius Therapeutics

LabGenius Therapeutics is a London-based drug discovery company that specializes in the use of machine learning and high-throughput experimentation to optimize complex therapeutic antibodies. The company's core technology platform, EVA™, integrates synthetic biology, robotics, and machine learning to design, conduct, and learn from experiments, enabling the rapid discovery of novel therapeutic assets.

## Mission and Focus

LabGenius's mission is to develop next-generation protein therapeutics. The company is particularly focused on the development of orally available drugs and has a project dedicated to the treatment of Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD). They are also working on a pipeline of solid tumor-targeting antibodies, including T-cell engagers (TCEs) and antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).

## Technology

The EVA™ platform is the core of LabGenius's drug discovery process. It is a smart robotic platform that is capable of designing, conducting, and learning from its own experiments. This allows the company to rapidly discover and optimize novel therapeutic antibodies.

## Team and Leadership

LabGenius has a highly multidisciplinary team that brings together experts from the fields of computer science, robotic automation, and synthetic biology. The company has also assembled a world-class scientific advisory team with expertise in early drug development, clinical translation, T-cell engagers, and antibody-drug conjugates.

## Funding and Partnerships

LabGenius has received funding from Innovate UK and has established partnerships with other companies in the biotechnology and pharmaceutical industries, including Sanofi. These collaborations allow LabGenius to leverage its sophisticated capabilities to enhance the discovery pipelines of its partners.
