### **ARTICLE I: THE STEWARDSHIP COMMITTEE**

| **The Guardian** | Risk Mitigation | Hold GLOBAL_QUENCH authority |

**Note:** Initial members will serve as **archetypal placeholders** until suitable individuals are identified and ratified.

#### **Section 1.02: Selection & Succession**
- Members serve **staggered 3-year terms** (3 members rotate annually).
- New members require **unanimous approval** of existing committee.
- Removal requires **7/9 supermajority** for cause.
- No member may serve more than **2 consecutive terms**.

#### **Section 1.03: Quorum & Voting**
- **Quorum:** 7 members (75%) must participate for binding decisions.
- **Emergency Quorum:** 5 members (55%) for GLOBAL_QUENCH activation.
- **Voting Window:** 45 minutes for standard decisions.
- **Voting Method:** Threshold cryptographic signatures (7-of-9).
